Frequently asked questions

How much does a consultation cost?

The fee depends only on the length: €40 for 15 minutes, €80 for 30 minutes, €100 for 45 minutes and €120 for the one-hour initial assessment. Online consultations follow exactly the same scale.

Will my health insurance reimburse me?

Partly. The amount varies between Belgian mutuelles and can go up to €25 per consultation. I am not conventionné, so reimbursement goes through your complementary insurance. I give you a care certificate at every consultation — that is the document you claim with.

Do I need a referral from my doctor?

No, a referral is not required to see me. It is still useful: with one, your mutuelle usually reimburses more sessions — typically between four and eight a year.

Do you consult online?

Yes, from anywhere in the world, in English, French or Italian. The fee is the same as for an in-person consultation.

How long does the follow-up last?

It depends on your situation. Sometimes a single consultation is enough to unlock things. More often, expect three to five appointments: my aim is to make you independent as quickly as possible.

Where do consultations take place?

At three addresses in Brussels: CHIREC — Delta Hospital (Boulevard du Triomphe 201, 1160 Auderghem), City Clinic Louise (Avenue Louise 235b, 1050 Ixelles) and the CEDAD Medical Center in Uccle (Rue Beeckman 71, 1180 Uccle). And online.

Is Low FODMAP a diet for life?

No — quite the opposite. The protocol runs in three phases: roughly four weeks of elimination, then a guided reintroduction food group by food group, and finally your own personalised, sustainable and as varied as possible way of eating.

I have my breath test results — can you help me make sense of them?

Yes. I help you read your result and understand what separates SIBO (hydrogen), IMO (methane) and ISO (hydrogen sulfide), and what each of them means in practice for the way you eat. This draws on my own practice and on my work alongside gastroenterologists and internal medicine specialists. The diagnosis itself belongs to your doctor: my role is to translate the result into food choices, not to make or change a diagnosis.

How do I pay, and what happens if I cancel?

Payment is by cash or QR code. For cancellations: free of charge more than 48 hours ahead, 50% of the fee between 48 and 24 hours, and the full fee under 24 hours or if you do not attend.
